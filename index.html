Research and engineering, together

My work connects physical models with reconstruction algorithms, statistical fitting and simulation-based uncertainty studies. During nearly seven years of doctoral research and scientific software work, I maintained the existing LuminosityFit framework and served as Software Owner for the PANDA Luminosity Detector Group.

I also develop PANDA Agent, extending this experience into scientific knowledge retrieval and code understanding. My AI-assisted workflow combines architecture and requirements ownership with integration, systematic evaluation, review and technical acceptance.
