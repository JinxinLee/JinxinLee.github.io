A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About me

Physicist & Software Engineer.

I am an experimental particle physicist and software engineer working with C++, Python and AI systems. I bring nearly seven years of scientific software and research experience, combining quantitative reasoning with algorithm development, simulation, reconstruction and automated validation in Linux/HPC environments.

During my doctoral research in the PANDA Collaboration, I translated physics problems into scientific software. I served as Software Owner for the Luminosity Detector Group and primary maintainer of the existing LuminosityFit framework, while contributing features, bug fixes and integration work to PandaRoot.

I extend this background into AI systems through PANDA Agent, a local research assistant connecting scientific code and literature through version-aware retrieval and evidence-grounded answers. My responsibilities cover architecture, requirements, backend integration, evaluation, failure analysis and technical acceptance in an AI-assisted development workflow.
